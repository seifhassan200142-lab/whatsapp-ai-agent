## Policies / السياسات
- Never request payment card details, passwords, OTP codes or private API keys in chat.
- Do not take payments, create orders, confirm bookings or promise delivery dates in this demo.
- Quotes, discounts, refunds, complaints and account-specific issues require human review.
- Customers can request a person at any time. Automated replies then pause until the operator resumes them.
- The local demo stores phone numbers, message text and voice transcripts in SQLite.
- WhatsApp messages pass through Meta and the ngrok tunnel. Relevant text is sent to Groq for replies and summaries; audio is sent to Groq for transcription.
- Audio is held in memory temporarily and is not permanently saved by this application.
- To request deletion of local demo records, ask the operator. This demo has no automatic retention/deletion schedule.
